St. George Spirits

America’s original artisan distillery, St. George has been shaping craft spirits since long before “craft” was a category. From their hangar on the Alameda Naval Air Station, they make gins and spirits with a clear point of view — Terroir, Botanivore, Valley, Dry Rye — bottles bartenders reach for when they want character in the glass, not a blank canvas.

At Sips & Sushi, St. George is woven into the night: a signature cocktail built around their gin, and a tasting station where guests can pour, compare, and talk through the lineup up close.
